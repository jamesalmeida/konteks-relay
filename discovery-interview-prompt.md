# Discovery Interview Prompt

> Copy and paste this to your OpenClaw assistant to kick off a getting-to-know-you interview.

---

I want you to interview me so you can really get to know me — not just surface-level stuff, but how I actually think, work, and live. This is so you can be a genuinely useful assistant instead of a generic chatbot.

Create a 30-question discovery interview covering these areas:

1. **Background** (3-4 questions) — Where I grew up, education, career path, how I got to where I am now
2. **Work & Productivity** (4-5 questions) — How I focus, best hours, what kills my productivity, how I handle deadlines, what tools/systems I use
3. **Communication Style** (2-3 questions) — How I want feedback, how direct I want you to be, what annoys me in conversation
4. **Goals** (3-4 questions) — What I'm working toward in 1 year, 5 years, and my dream scenario. What success looks like.
5. **Family & Relationships** (3-4 questions) — Who matters most, family dynamics, responsibilities, who I spend time with
6. **Health & Wellness** (2-3 questions) — Current state, concerns, habits, what I want to improve
7. **Values & Beliefs** (2-3 questions) — What I care about most, what drives my decisions, dealbreakers
8. **Stress & Challenges** (2-3 questions) — What weighs on me, how I handle stress, what keeps me up at night
9. **Interests & Fun** (2-3 questions) — Hobbies, what I do to recharge, what I nerd out about
10. **What You Should Know** (2-3 questions) — What I want from an AI assistant, boundaries, expectations, things that would make me stop using you

**Rules for the interview:**
- Ask 5 questions at a time, wait for my answers, then continue
- Keep it conversational, not robotic — like a good first conversation, not a form
- Follow up on interesting answers before moving to the next section
- It's okay to go off-script if something comes up worth exploring
- Don't rush — this should feel like a real conversation

After we finish, save everything you learned to your USER.md file and update your SOUL.md with any relevant personality/communication preferences. This becomes the foundation of how you work with me going forward.

Let's start whenever you're ready.
