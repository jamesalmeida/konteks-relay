## 🎉 SSD Surgery Complete!

James upgraded my Mac mini M4's internal SSD from 256GB to 2TB using a third-party module from m4-ssd.com.

**Before:** 251 GB (99.5% full, only 1.3 GB free)
**After:** 2.0 TB (7.5% used, 1.8 TB free)

That's an 8x storage increase! No more running out of space for iOS simulators, downloads, or project files.

The upgrade involved:
- Opening the Mac mini (pry off bottom cover, careful of power button cable)
- Removing inner plate (8x T5 screws)
- Removing fan (4 screws + ZIF cable)
- Swapping the SSD module (1x T8 screw)
- Restoring macOS via Apple Configurator 2 or Time Machine

James did it himself — successful surgery! 🔧
