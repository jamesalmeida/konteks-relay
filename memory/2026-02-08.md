# 2026-02-08 - FastClaw iOS App Development

## Project Rename: Clawpernicus → FastClaw

James renamed the iOS companion app for OpenClaw from "Clawpernicus" to **FastClaw**.

**FastClaw value prop:** Fast, simple iOS access to OpenClaw. Scan QR code, done. No VPN, no Tailscale, no port forwarding.

## Key Architecture Decision: Convex as Relay

Important clarification from James: **Convex IS the relay layer** between OpenClaw Gateway and FastClaw app.

```
OpenClaw Gateway → Convex ← FastClaw App
    (pushes)      (relay)   (subscribes)
```

This is what enables "scan QR and it works from anywhere" - both sides connect outbound to Convex, no firewall issues.

**I initially misunderstood** and said "Convex for accounts only, not conversations" - that was WRONG. Conversations DO flow through Convex. That's the whole point - it's the relay that makes simple QR pairing possible.

1. Dashboard - OpenClaw status at a glance
